Report kind: return brief. The captain is back after an away window. The FACTS are the return brief in its fixed order.
Keep every section of it, in the same order, each with its own `##` heading. Shorten each section to what the captain must know.
Say plainly what the captain asked for while away and what was done under it, in the captain's own words where the FACTS quote them.
Never drop a blocker, a decision waiting on the captain, or a failure.
