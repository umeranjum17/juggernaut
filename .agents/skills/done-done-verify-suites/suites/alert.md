# Alert table

Alert table: run `done-done run alert` (`bash journeys/alert.sh`, which runs `tests/jg-alert.test.sh`, `tests/jg-task-inbox-alert.test.sh` and `core/undelivered-alert/tests/undelivered-alert.test.sh` and writes its log and `steps.json` into the run's folder); it lints `core/machines/tables/alert.tsv` (5 states, 8 moves), calls the real `bin/jg-alert.sh` in a fixture home, raises a stuck wait in each of the four delivery modes (one P2 alert at rung 2), keeps quiet rows quiet, raises none for a home with `supervision-down` open and releases them when it resolves, gates the fires-at time by the limit of its kind, climbs the ladder to its top with rung 4 held while away, repeats at the top three times and then makes the alert stuck with one escalation-stuck card, rings each of the new rows (wrong-repo, lock-held, orphan-process, escalation-stuck, fleet-blocked, quota-unused, quota-blind) and the 4 h kind of stranded, drives open, acknowledged, inhibited and resolved, delivers a ring to its holder as one wake (a tick ring is only recorded), and raises `undelivered` once from an unread steer on a dead endpoint (no stale wake) and from a refused captain message; it reads only generated fixtures (screen: none).
