# Spawns overlap under the project lock

Run `done-done run -- bash -c 'bash tests/jg-spawn-overlap.test.sh > "$DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT/spawn-overlap.log" 2>&1'` with the pinned Beads test binaries in `JG_BEADS_TEST_BD` and `JG_BEADS_TEST_DOLT`.
The real brief and spawn commands over the real worktree tool and a stand-in terminal whose pane reaches its copy 2 s after the `cd` start eight spawns of one project at once.
Each spawn's phase log shows every mark its path takes, no two spawns hold the project lock together, at least two wait for their pane together, and all eight records exist within 15 s, each on its own copy with its own owner claim.
A pane that never settles fails its spawn after the lock is let go, returns the copy, and the next spawn of the one-copy pool takes it; a project with a declared capacity keeps the lock until its record is published (screen: none).
