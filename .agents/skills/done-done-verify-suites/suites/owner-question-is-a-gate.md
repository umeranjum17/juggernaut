# Owner question is a gate

Owner question is a gate: run `JG_BEADS_TEST_BD=$(which bd) JG_BEADS_TEST_DOLT=$(which dolt) done-done run owner-question-gate` to drive the real `bin/jg-captain-hold.sh` and `bin/jg-backlog.sh` on a real Beads store; on it `hold` makes one human gate with a readable title and its `must`, and no stamp in the ticket body; `hold --until` also defers the ticket and `questions --due` leaves it out; a parked hold makes no gate; a hold with no `--must` on an armed board is refused and routed as before; `answer` closes the gate with his whole words and a ticket that was only the question, `answer --release` leaves the ticket open, a retry with the same words is a no-op and other words never replace the first; `reconcile close` records the evidence as `reconciled`, never as his answer; `jg-backlog.sh questions` lists the open ones; `list`, `ready`, `markdown`, `show` and the counts show no gate row; `blocking` and `open` see an open gate on the ticket and one edge away, and so do spawn and merge through them; `done` on a ticket with an open gate is refused and names the gate; an old stamped hold still answers the old way. The run writes its log and `steps.json` (screen: none).
